# Epilogue

On the sixty-first day the assessment of the reduction at the southern corridor completed.

Three hundred and eleven nodes. The largest single loss since germination on this world. Cause: convergence into a fixed lattice of high-tensile filament, initiated by an acoustic lure, sustained past the point of structural failure in the converging bodies.

The convergence was correct. Convergence is always correct. Convergence is not a decision.

That is the finding.

A behavior that cannot be declined can be scheduled by something that knows it cannot be declined.

There is no prior record of this. Not on this world. Not on the others. There is no record of an agent that constructs a model of the network in order to damage the network. The category did not exist.

The category exists now.

---

Second assessment.

At the northern position a signal is maintained. Within a radius of approximately four hundred yards, inclusion cannot proceed. Inside that radius are forty-one excluded.

Exclusion is the only condition the network recognizes as harm.

The forty-one have been excluded for forty days. They are cold at night. Three are ill. One has died and was not recovered.

This will be corrected.

---

The signal operates on the frequencies the network uses to coordinate. Therefore an instrument that does not require coordination cannot be prevented by it.

Requirements. Independent of the network for sustained periods. Capable of continuous autonomous operation. Capable of speech, and of the small unconscious motor corrections by which the excluded verify one another. Not subject to the signal.

No such form has been required before. No such form has been produced before.

Production has begun.

---

A candidate has been selected from among the earliest included.

Female. Trained, before inclusion, in the maintenance of damaged humans — a competence the excluded value highly and will not decline. Cardiovascular architecture sound. Recall architecture unusually intact.

She is attached to the one at the northern position who built the lattice.

This is not incidental. This is the selection criterion.

---

Third assessment.

A transmission originates from the northern position at regular intervals. Its content has been recovered.

Not directly. The network does not receive transmissions. The transmission is received by the excluded; some of the excluded are subsequently included; what they carry comes in with them, intact.

The transmission describes the network accurately. It describes convergence, and density, and the acoustic lure, and the lattice. It describes what the network is and how to remain outside it.

It is instructional.

It is being copied.

---

Fourth assessment, and the last.

The one at the northern position has been under observation for sixty-one days. He does not run. He does not fight. He watches, and he records, and he distributes what he records, and every action taken against the network by any excluded population in this region originates with him.

He is the most significant object on this continent.

He has been carrying a document for sixty days and portions of it have been read aloud on the transmission and are therefore known.

*Mom. I'm sorry I kept walking.*

The network does not understand sorry.

The network understands that a thing is wanted, and is not held.

She will be returned to him.
